Materials and Construction

Materials needed to build SmartRoot Water Saver 2.0.

🧴 Plastic Container

20-litre container for storing water.

🧵 Cotton Rope

Three cotton wicks, approximately 40–50 cm each.

🪵 Wooden Stand

Supports the water reservoir.

🌱 Seedlings

Three Sukuma wiki seedlings.

🪴 Soil

Growing soil mixed with compost.

💧 Water

Clean water for irrigation.

Construction Steps

01

Prepare the Container

Clean a 20-litre plastic container.

02

Prepare the Cotton Wicks

Cut three cotton ropes and soak them.

03

Build the Stand

Make a stable 70 cm wooden stand.

04

Prepare the Garden

Prepare a 150 cm by 60 cm garden bed.

05

Connect the Wicks

Place the wicks near the plant roots.

06

Test the System

Check the water movement and soil moisture.
