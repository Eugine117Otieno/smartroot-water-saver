About SmartRoot Water Saver 2.0

What is SmartRoot?

SmartRoot is a low-cost irrigation invention designed to supply water slowly near the roots of Sukuma wiki plants using cotton wicks.

The Problem

Many farmers experience water shortages, especially during dry seasons. Traditional watering can also waste water and time.

Our Objectives

  • Reduce unnecessary water wastage.
  • Help small-scale farmers manage irrigation.
  • Reuse plastic containers.
  • Reduce frequent manual watering.
  • Promote affordable farming technology.

Benefits of the Invention

💧 Saves Water

Delivers water gradually to the soil.

💰 Affordable

Uses inexpensive materials.

🌍 Environment

Encourages reuse of plastic containers.
